Currently, I work as a Software Developer as part of the R&D team at <i>**Mimic Technologies, Inc. (part of [Surgical Science Sweden AB](https://surgicalscience.com/))**</i>, where I develop and maintain high‑performance real‑time graphics and simulation systems using C++, proprietary engines, Unity3D, & Nvidia PhysX for high‑fidelity [robotic surgery simulation](https://surgicalscience.com/medtech-solutions/
robotic-surgery-simulation/) products across multiple platforms.

</br>

## What I Do
I build real-time graphics, simulation, and engine systems—designing performant tools, rendering pipelines, and interactive software that brings complex systems to life. I enjoy solving hard technical problems, modernizing legacy systems, and creating workflows that make developers more effective.

## Areas of Focus
- Real-time graphics & simulation
- Engine, tools, and runtime systems
- Performance optimization (CPU/GPU, memory)
- Developer workflows (architecture, debugging, CI/CD)
- Experimentation & R&D in rendering techniques
